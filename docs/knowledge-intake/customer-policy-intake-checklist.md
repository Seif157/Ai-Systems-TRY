# Customer-policy intake checklist

- Confirm the customer database and authoritative policy owner independently of submitted metadata.
- Require exact customer and legal-entity scope, effective dates, modules, permissions, purpose,
  classification, approval identity, and approval timestamp.
- Verify withdrawal and supersession state before publication.
- Keep customer policy isolated from global product documentation and every other customer.
- Obtain human relevance judgements before production retrieval approval.
