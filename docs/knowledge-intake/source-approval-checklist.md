# Knowledge-source approval checklist

- Identify the authoritative document owner and controlled source.
- Record approval identity and timestamp; typed fields do not prove a human signature or the
  authenticity of an approval service.
- Confirm effective and withdrawal dates, module and permission scope, legal entities, customer
  ownership, classification, and employee-facing purpose.
- Reject transactional rows, schema engineering documents, credentials, and highly restricted data.
- Pin the reviewed bytes by SHA-256 and retain supersession history.
